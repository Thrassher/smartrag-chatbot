EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — SAMPLE DOCUMENT
LEAVE POLICY
Full-time employees may request up to 20 days of annual leave per year. Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year. Casual leave is limited to 5 days per year.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager. Employees should submit planned annual-leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.
REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Permanent remote work is not permitted under this policy.
BENEFITS AND SUPPORT
Eligible full-time employees receive health insurance after completing 30 days of employment. For payroll questions, employees should contact the HR operations team. The policy does not specify a transport allowance.
